FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my data used to train AI models?
Never. Your workspace data is encrypted and isolated to your tenant, and it is excluded from any model training by contract — on every plan, including Starter. You can also enable zero-retention mode so prompts and outputs are discarded after each response.
Which AI models does Nexara use?
Nexara is model-agnostic. A routing layer sends each task to the model that best fits cost, latency, and capability — and you can pin specific providers or self-hosted models per workflow if your compliance team requires it.
What counts as an "AI action"?
One action = one completed unit of work, like filing a ticket, summarising a thread, or drafting a document. Reading and searching your knowledge base is always free and unlimited on every plan.
How long does setup actually take?
Median time to first useful workflow is under 4 minutes: connect a tool, pick a template, done. Deeper rollouts — custom guardrails, SSO, data residency — typically take a day with our team's help.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no lock-in, and every plan carries a 30-day money-back guarantee. Export everything — workflows, logs, documents — whenever you want.
